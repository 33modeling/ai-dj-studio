# Original free practice music

The six MP3 files listed in `audio/catalog.json` are original electronic compositions
created for 33modeling with `scripts/create-free-music.py`. They use synthesized
oscillators and deterministic noise, with no third-party recordings or samples.

33modeling dedicates these six recordings and compositions to the public domain
under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
You may download, mix, modify, perform and redistribute them, including commercially.
Attribution is welcome and is not required for these bundled tracks.

This dedication applies only to the six bundled original MP3 files. Imported user
audio retains its own rights and licensing conditions. Each exported setlist includes
the known track names, source and license metadata.
